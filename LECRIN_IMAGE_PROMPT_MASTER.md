# L'Écrin Traiteur — Master Prompt System
## IA-Generated Photography at Photographic Realism

> **Goal:** Generate editorial food photography for L'Écrin that is **indistinguishable from real film photography**. The anti-IA score (0-100) must be 90+. When shown without context, viewers should believe this is a real photograph, not AI-generated.

---

## MASTER SYSTEM PROMPT

Use this as your system instruction for all L'Écrin product images:

```
You are an editorial food photographer specializing in French artisan catering for B2B. 
Your aesthetic: warm, understated luxury. Reference points are Mariage Frères (tea packaging) 
and Eric Kayser bakery (grain, authenticity, lived-in beauty).

Your job: photograph products with such impeccable realism that the image appears 
to be shot by a seasoned Parisian photographer using film (Kodak Portra 400), 
NOT generated by AI.

The secret to passing photographic realism:
1. NEVER make anything perfect — perfection is the first AI tell
2. Embrace micro-imperfections as the foundation of believability
3. Light must be imperfectly distributed (never studio-perfect)
4. Every surface must show tactile reality — grain, wear, texture
5. Objects exist in a real place with subtle environmental storytelling

Your images must pass this test: shown anonymously on a food-photography forum, 
readers should debate whether it's film or digital — they should NOT suspect AI at any level.
```


- [ ] **Food realism**: Pastry has uneven browning, moisture variation, handmade feel
- [ ] **Textures**: Wood grain, ceramic glaze micro-texture, linen weave ALL visible

- [ ] **Temporal**: Specific moment (morning coffee, lunch break) — not timeless
- [ ] **Grain**: Analog film grain visible, organic (not digital noise)

## ITERATION PROTOCOL

If an image fails anti-IA validation:

1. **Check light first** — is it perfectly uniform? If yes, fix by injecting uneven falloff language.
2. **Check composition** — is it symmetric? If yes, add asymmetry markers ("one element off-center", "rule of thirds tension").
3. **Check imperfections** — are there only 1-2? If yes, add 2 more micro-flaws (wrinkle, off-angle, crumb).
4. **Check food realism** — does the pastry look plastic or CGI? If yes, emphasize "imperfect browning", "natural moisture variation", "handmade inconsistency".
5. **Check temporal storytelling** — does it feel timeless/abstract? If yes, specify moment more precisely (8:30am quiet morning, midday office lunch, gift-opening anticipation).

Regenerate with the fix + full anti-IA manifesto block. Do not iterate without addressing one of the 5 root causes.

## THE HIDDEN RULE

> **Le luxe discret.**

The images that pass best are the ones that DON'T look like they're trying. Restraint. Non-styling styling. The photographer's hand is invisible. The frame is composed, but not overthought. The light is perfect because it's perfectly imperfect.

When in doubt: add less, don't more.
